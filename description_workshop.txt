[h1]Poland Mod [/h1]

Poland can into Terraria! Poland Mod adds new bosses, items, weapons, buffs, and more inspired by Polish culture.


[h2]Features[/h2]
- [b]New Bosses:[/b] Face the mighty Eagle Boss and more!
- [b]Unique Weapons:[/b] Try the Potato Cannon, Bober Staff, and more.
- [b]Polish Items:[/b] Enjoy Pierogi, Vodka, Polish Flag, and other themed items.
- [b]Buffs & Debuffs:[/b] Bober Buff, Vodka Debuff, and more effects.
- [b]Custom Tiles & Relics:[/b]Decorate your world with Polish relics and flags.
- [b]Plants[/b]: Plant and grow new potato herbs to craft new items.



[h2]How to Install[/h2]
1. Download and enable Poland Mod in tModLoader.
2. Launch Terraria and start your Polish adventure!
