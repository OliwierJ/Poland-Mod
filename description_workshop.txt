## PolandMod

Experience Terraria with a Polish twist! PolandMod adds new bosses, items, weapons, buffs, and more inspired by Polish culture.


### Features
- **New Bosses:** Face the mighty EagleBoss and more!
- **Unique Weapons:** Try the PotatoCannon, BoberStaff, and more.
- **Polish Items:** Enjoy Pierogi, Vodka, PolishFlag, and other themed items.
- **Buffs & Debuffs:** BoberBuff, VodkaDebuff, and more effects.
- **Custom Tiles & Relics:** Decorate your world with Polish relics and flags.



### How to Install
1. Download and enable PolandMod in tModLoader.
2. Launch Terraria and start your Polish adventure!
